Why these defaults. Every buy is at least $50, then +$5 after a win, and back to $50 after a loss. The bot never shrinks a buy to fit. There is no fixed take-profit. At +15% a trade starts riding the peak and sells only when momentum fades: the tighter of a 9-point giveback or one third of the peak, and never gives the locked +5% back on purpose. A sharp red 5-minute candle or sellers taking over also exits. Stops stay at -8% for scalps and -20% for swings. Set "extra app fee" if the phone app you copy charges one.
What this cannot make realistic. Prices are DexScreener quotes a few seconds old, so a wick between checks is invisible. There is no private mempool, no sandwich bot racing you, and no true on-chain stop that fills at the trigger price. Honeypots are only caught when RugCheck flags mint, freeze, or a tax. Holder data is missing when RugCheck does not answer, and those coins are not blocked. The dip uses the high this app has seen plus the 5 minute, 1 hour, 6 hour, and 1 day moves, not every candle. The app only trades while this page is open. Nothing here is financial advice.
Where coins come from. DexScreener's public Solana boost and new-token lists, the same sources as the other simulator. Fomo does not publish its list. This app never reads or writes the other app's saved balance.
