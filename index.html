Real-world effects

All on by default. They make the fake money behave like real money, mostly in ways that cost you. Each one shows its reason in Activity.
Rug pulls and dead coinsLiquidity vanishes, the price gaps to almost nothing and you get back little or nothing.
Execution delayOrders fill 2-5 seconds after the decision, at the newer price.
Stop-loss gapsIf the price jumps past the stop, you sell at the worse price, not exactly -20%.
Failed transactionsAbout 4-12% of orders fail and retry on the next check. The network fee is still charged.
Smarter slippageBigger orders on thin pools, and fast-moving prices, cost more.
Priority tip feeAn extra $0.05-0.20 per trade on busy coins.
Sell pressureSelling while most traders are selling costs extra slippage.
RugCheck safety checkSkips coins RugCheck flags as high risk. Works from this page.

Keep screen on while open
Please read. This only trades while the app is open on your screen. When you close it or lock your phone, it stops looking at prices and makes no buys or sells. When you open it again it checks prices right then, so a coin may have gone past the stop in the meantime and sells at the new, worse price.
What is NOT simulated: a real wallet or real money (none is connected), the real order book and exact pool depth, other traders and bots racing or sandwiching your orders, coins you cannot sell at all (honeypots) or that take a cut of every transfer, holder spread and linked wallets, and whether a coin's story and socials are real. RugCheck is a quick screen, not a guarantee. Prices come from DexScreener and can be a few seconds old. Real trades can do worse than this. The "dip from high" uses prices this app has seen plus DexScreener's 5 minute, 1 hour, 6 hour and 1 day moves, so it is a good guess, not the full chart.
How the fake money acts like real money: each buy and sell pays a platform fee, a small network fee, and slippage (the price moves against you, more on small pools). Cash goes down on a buy and comes back on a sell. Real trades can still fill worse, fail, or get stuck on thin or fake coins. Nothing here is advice or a promise.
Where coins come from: DexScreener's public lists for Solana (newly boosted, newly listed and most boosted coins). Fomo has no public list this app can read, so the Trending tab is labelled "Trending (Solana)" and is not Fomo's own list.
