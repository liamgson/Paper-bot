Please read. This only trades while the app is open on your screen. When you close it or lock your phone, it stops looking at prices and makes no buys or sells. When you open it again it checks prices right then, so a coin may have gone past the stop in the meantime and sells at the new, worse price.
Not checked here: top holder over 4%, wallets holding the same amounts (bundles), and whether the story and socials are real. Those need RugCheck or a look at the coin yourself. The "dip from high" uses prices this app has seen plus DexScreener's 5 minute, 1 hour, 6 hour and 1 day moves, so it is a good guess, not the full chart.
How the fake money is treated like real money: each buy and sell pays a platform fee, a small network fee, and slippage (the price moves against you, more on small pools). Cash goes down on a buy and comes back on a sell. Real trades can still fill worse, fail, or get stuck on thin or fake coins. Nothing here is advice or a promise.
